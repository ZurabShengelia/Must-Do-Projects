Privacy

This site has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies, and it has no server of its own.

Location

Your location is requested only when you press “My Location”. It is used only to show the weather and the place name. It is not stored on any server and is not saved in your browser.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo receives the city text you search for, or the coordinates of the place, to return city matches and the forecast.
  • BigDataCloud receives your coordinates together with your IP address when you use “My Location”, to return a place name. BigDataCloud says it uses this pair anonymously to improve its IP-geolocation data. This site does not link it to your identity.

Both services are free and need no key. Weather data: Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0. Like any website request, these services can see your IP address.

Stored in your browser

Theme, language, temperature unit, last city and up to five recent cities are kept in your browser’s local storage. They stay on your device. Clear your browser data to remove them.
